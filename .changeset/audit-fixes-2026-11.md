A shard that is not ready leaves `guilds` out of its heartbeat entry, so a restart's first beat can no longer zero the public server count; `ShardReport.guilds` is optional accordingly. `getServerCount` only runs on the elected publisher, so a large bot no longer exhausts its budget at startup. A commands post that fails on a `5xx`, a network error, a rate limit or a Discord fetch tries again every 5 minutes until it lands or gets a `4xx` retrying cannot fix. A heartbeat `429` no longer suspends stats and commands. With an explicit `botId` the topology is re-detected on ready, so `shards: 'auto'` reports its real shard count. Stats on indomitable sum its clusters. `minGap` can no longer go below 60s. The webhook handlers survive a throwing `onError` and a non-ASCII signature. A heartbeat loop survives a tick landing during `postHeartbeat()` and restarts after re-arming a disabled publisher, a throwing event listener no longer ends a publisher's loop, and nothing re-arms after `stop()`. `client.destroy()` now stops the poster, which used to keep beating `connecting` for as long as the process lived. `stop()` also ends a retry wait, which now reports `stopped`, so nothing is posted after it. A commands `429` that outlasts the 5-minute retry answers at once instead of waiting inside `postCommands()`, and a `postCommands()` that lands ends a pending retry.
